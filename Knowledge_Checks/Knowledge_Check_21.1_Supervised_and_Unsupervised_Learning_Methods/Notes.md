# Question 9

## Which of the following two-step processes is used in the K-Means algorithm?

### Correct Answer

✅ The expectation-maximization process

### Notes

K-Means repeatedly performs:

## Expectation Step

Assign observations to the closest centroid.

## Maximization Step

Recalculate centroid positions.

```text
Expectation
    ↓
Assign Points
    ↓
Maximization
    ↓
Update Centroids
    ↓
Repeat Until Convergence
```
