One of the most helpful resources I found during this module is the official TensorFlow documentation:

https://www.tensorflow.org/tutorials

TensorFlow was introduced during the Deep Neural Networks section of the module and provided practical examples that helped me better understand neural network concepts such as input layers, hidden layers, output layers, activation functions, forward propagation, and model training. The tutorials also helped me connect the MNIST handwritten digit recognition example from the lesson to real-world implementations using TensorFlow and Keras.

Another resource I found useful is the OpenAI Gymnasium documentation:

https://gymnasium.farama.org/

This resource helped me better understand reinforcement learning environments such as Taxi-v4 and Frozen Lake. The examples and environment documentation made it easier to understand states, actions, rewards, Q-Learning, and how the Bellman Equation is applied when training reinforcement learning agents.

A helpful tip for this module is to visualize reinforcement learning problems as maps or graphs. Drawing the states and actions on paper made it much easier for me to understand how the Q-Matrix stores rewards and how the agent learns the optimal path through repeated exploration and exploitation.

I plan to bookmark both resources because they provide excellent examples and reference material that will be useful for future machine learning, reinforcement learning, and deep learning projects.
